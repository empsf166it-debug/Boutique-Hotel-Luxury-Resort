Design & Architecture

Designed to Feel Different.

Aurelle Retreat was conceived not as a monument, but as an architectural shelter sculpted into the limestone cliffs, in dialogue with the sky, sunlight, and quietude.

Honoring Natural Terroir & Tactile Stillness

Every wall has been finished with hand-troweled lime plaster and native travertine quarry stones cut on site. Unlacquered brass fixtures, natural linen drapes, and oiled oak invite a tactile relationship between human presence and organic architecture.

Biophilic Design

Floor-to-ceiling pocketing doors disappear entirely into the walls, allowing sea breezes to circulate freely and blurring the boundary between living room and horizon.
